# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

NovaGhost is dedicated to providing an open, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, gender identity, or sexual orientation.

We are committed to building an inclusive space where students, educators, and software engineers can collaborate, learn, and contribute openly.

---

## Standards of Behavior

Participants in this project are expected to:

- Demonstrate empathy and respect toward others.
- Be thoughtful when giving and receiving constructive technical feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community, especially students seeking accessible education tools.
- Respect differing viewpoints, approaches, and coding styles.
- Maintain professional communication in issue trackers, discussions, and code reviews.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassing, insulting, or derogatory comments.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or political disruptions.
- Publishing private personal information of contributors without consent.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional context.

---

## Responsibilities & Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate or harmful.

Maintainers have the authority to edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and direct project-related interactions.
